About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer/Data Scientist specializing in Spatio-Temporal Systems and Machine Learning. My Ph.D. work at Iowa State University bridges the gap between innovative high-performance engineering and ML, focusing on designing efficient and scalable architectures for complex, petabyte-scale datasets to enable real-world applications and derive meaningful insights.


My research has led to novel algorithms for spatio-temporal data management, including the SDC framework and BARMC algorithm, which have been published in top-tier venues like IEEE MDM, ACM SIGSPATIAL, ACM TSAS and Information Systems. With a strong foundation in distributed systems and big data technologies, I have developed scalable ML pipelines that can handle millions of data points while optimizing for performance and accuracy.


During my time at Altair Engineering (now Siemens), I optimized GNN and Transformer architectures to achieve a significant reduction in MAE for large-scale physical simulations. With a toolkit spanning Python, PyTorch, Spark, and C++, I build ML systems that scale to millions of data points. Whether architecting Agentic RAG workflows or optimizing GPU-accelerated inference, I am driven by turning high-dimensional, noisy data into actionable intelligence.
